Privacy Policy

Notch Ball (the game) · Effective 22 October 2026

The short version. Notch Ball has no accounts and no servers of ours. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your progress (coins, unlocked items, story level, settings) stays on your phone. A few well-known services we use – for ads, purchases and crash reports – receive limited technical data, described below.

1. Who we are

The game Notch Ball is published by Dheeraj Kumar, an individual developer based in Portugal ("we", "us"), who is also the controller of any personal data described here. You can contact us at spicatech1@gmail.com.

2. What the game keeps on your device

To remember your progress the game saves a small file in the app's private storage on your phone: your coins, the items you have unlocked, the table, ball and basket you use, your story level and stars, your settings (sound, aim style, emojis), your tickets and a made-up player name such as "Player4821". This file is not sent to us and we cannot read it. It is protected against being edited by hand. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. If you have Android's device backup switched on, Android itself may copy the app's data to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone; that is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, health data, or your precise or approximate location. The game does not ask for those permissions.

5. Opponents in the game

In CHALLENGE and STORY the opponents you see, with their names and levels, are computer-controlled players, not other people. No information about you is sent to any opponent. There is no chat and no multiplayer service.

6. Children

Notch Ball is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age set by the law where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

7. How long data is kept, and deleting it

Data on your device stays until you delete it by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. Data held by the services in section 4 is kept under their own policies. To ask about data those services hold about your device, write to us and we will help you reach the right provider; you can also reset your advertising ID in your phone settings.

8. Security

The saved progress is sealed so it cannot be edited by hand, purchases are verified through Google Play and RevenueCat, and the services above use encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep the game safe.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or limit its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, www.cnpd.pt, or the authority of the country where you live). Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, most of these choices are made in your device and Google settings or with the providers in section 4. For anything else, contact us at the address above.

10. International transfers

The providers in section 4 may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the game or on its store page when the change is important.
