Notch Ball

The cluster ball marble game with real physics. Pull back like a slingshot, aim, and roll your marble up a tilted table into the notches.

Get it on Google Play

How it plays

  • Pull down for power, sideways to aim. A glowing guide shows the path.
  • Three rows of notch baskets worth 1, 2 and 3 points. The far row pays the most.
  • Pot a ball and shoot again. Pot several in a row and the points count double.
  • Two 3-minute rounds per match, with sides swapped for round two.

Modes

  • Challenge: face computer-controlled rivals on random tables and baskets.
  • Story: a long road of levels, each on its own basket.
  • Singles: practice on your own table, ball and basket.
  • 2 Players: pass the phone and play a friend on one device.

Collect and customise

Ball skins, table themes and basket layouts: start with two free of each kind and earn the rest with the coins you win.
